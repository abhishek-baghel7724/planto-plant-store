A good place to start

Choose a plant that fits your life

01

Start with your space

Notice where you want greenery: a desk, a shelf, an empty corner, or a bright window. The spot helps guide the size and shape to look for.

02

Pick your style

Choose the look you enjoy. Full leafy shapes feel lush, while upright plants and succulents bring a clean, sculptural touch.

03

Think about your routine

Consider how much time you want to spend caring for a plant. A simple routine can make it easier to enjoy your new green companion.

A simple plant-picking guide

Let your room lead the way.

There is no single “right” plant for every home. Start with the place you have and the feeling you want to create.

For a work desk

Look for a compact shape that leaves you room to work.

For a quiet corner

Choose a taller silhouette or a fuller plant to make the space feel considered.

For a shelf

Try a small plant whose shape and pot complement the objects around it.
